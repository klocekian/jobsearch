This policy covers the Job Search web app at https://jobs.fieldlines.org (the "Service") and the

## What we collect

**Account information.** When you sign in with Google we receive your name and email address. We
don't receive your Google password or have access to other Google data.

**What you save in the Service.** This includes:
- Jobs you add: company, title, location, salary, the posting's URL and description, status,
  notes, and anything you record about your applications.
- Resumes and candidate documents you upload or write.
- The application details on your Profile page: name, email, phone, address, city, state,
  current title and employer, profile links, and your answers about work authorization and
  sponsorship.
- API keys for AI providers you choose to connect.

**What the Extension handles.**
- *Clip:* when you click "Clip this page", the Extension reads the posting on the current tab:
  its URL, title, meta description, structured job data, and visible text (or only the text you
  selected). This goes to your account only when you click "Save Job" or "Re-extract with AI".
- *Fill:* when you open the Fill tab or click "Fill application fields", the Extension downloads
  your saved application details from your account and enters them into the form on the current
  tab. It doesn't read or send anything else you type into that form, and it never submits it.
- *Already-saved check:* when the side panel opens, it downloads your saved jobs and compares the
  current tab's address with them on your device. That address isn't sent to us.
- *Settings:* the Extension keeps two preferences on your device, in Chrome's local storage: the
  server address and whether the recent-clips list is collapsed.

The Extension does nothing on a page until you click a button. It doesn't track your browsing,
record clicks or keystrokes, show ads, or include analytics.

## How we use it

Only to provide the Service: storing your job search, showing it back to you, analyzing your fit
for roles, drafting resumes and cover letters, and filling applications when you ask. We don't
sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to decide on credit or lending.

## AI processing

Some features send text to an AI provider: extracting fields from a posting, fit checks, resume
rewrites and cover letters. The request includes the posting text and, for features that need
it, your resume or profile text. It goes to the provider you connected under Profile → AI
(Anthropic, Google Gemini, xAI Grok or Mistral). If you haven't connected one, it may go to a
provider we pay for. The provider handles the request under its own terms and privacy policy.
These calls happen only when you use an AI feature.

If you connect Claude, ChatGPT or another assistant to your account (through MCP), that assistant
can read and change your job search data on your behalf. You can disconnect it at any time from
your Profile.

## Who else handles your data

- **Hosting and storage:** the Service runs on Vercel, and its database is hosted by Turso.
- **Sign-in:** Google, for authentication.
- **AI providers:** as described above.

We don't share your data with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

## Security

All traffic between the Extension, your browser and the Service uses HTTPS. Every record belongs
to one account and is shown only to that account's owner. No method of storing or sending data
is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect yours.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays in your account until you delete it. You can delete individual jobs and
resumes, and disconnect AI providers and connected apps, at any time. To delete your whole
account and everything in it, email [CONTACT EMAIL]. We'll do it within 30 days. Uninstalling the
Extension removes its local settings.

## Children

The Service isn't meant for anyone under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data.
